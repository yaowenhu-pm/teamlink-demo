# TeamLink v0.6 · Brand and website direction

User goal: an Apple-inspired level of craft, while keeping direct landscape editing and phone sharing one step away. Preserve the existing shared-room server and model adapter.

Visual direction: warm porcelain page, ink typography, glacial/cobalt blue identity, oversized restrained headlines, generous spacing, one real interactive landscape as the main product image. Refined ambient illustration, a compact toolbar and a clear composer. Controller links open the scene directly.

Logo asset: built-in image_gen, logo-brand, new transparent brand symbol. Final prompt: “Create one exceptional new logo symbol for TeamLink, a calm collaborative creation tool. A single isolated symbol on a truly transparent background: two interlocking rounded-square ribbon links, offset diagonally, connected with a graceful central over-under weave. Recognizable open negative spaces, balanced proportions, a simple silhouette, front view with a slight dimensional bevel. Polished ceramic blue to glacial blue, pearl highlights, controlled depth, crisp edges. No text, tile, extra symbols or background.”

Production assets: web/assets/teamlink-symbol.png (800 px), web/assets/teamlink-icon.png (128 px). Original generated image retained in the Codex generated_images folder. Asset downsizing only; no generative edits after the selected image.

Acceptance: desktop/mobile composition, logo clarity, no horizontal overflow, keyboard/focus/escape, reduced motion, real same-room direct editing and model flow. No credentials in frontend. Keep legacy routes and room fragments.
